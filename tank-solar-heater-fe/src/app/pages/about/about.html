Our mission

Endless hot water shouldn't cost the earth

We started SunTank in a garage in 2009 with a simple frustration: water heating was wasteful, ugly and expensive. So we engineered systems that put the sun to work first, backed by smart storage tanks for the moments it can't.

Today we've fitted more than twelve thousand homes with solar collectors, storage tanks and heat-pump hybrids — each one quietly cutting energy use without anyone giving up a single hot shower.
